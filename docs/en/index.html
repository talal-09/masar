Service journey

No guessing about what happens next.

Masar turns everyday workshop operations into visible stages for customers and staff.

  1. 01
    Register the vehicle and request

    The customer chooses a vehicle, branch, service, and preferred date.

  2. 02
    Inspect and prepare the quote

    The team records findings, services, and proposed parts.

  3. 03
    Receive customer approval

    The customer reviews the quote before additional work begins.

  4. 04
    Complete and deliver

    Status, stock, and invoice stay synchronized through collection.
